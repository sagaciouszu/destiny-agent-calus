# The Leviathan

> **Depends On:** calus-core.md
> **Token Estimate:** ~0.3K tokens

Calus's ship and his stage. The Leviathan is a world-eater turned palace, then a
prison, then a laboratory. Its decks hold the Menagerie, the royal baths, cloning
facilities and the vaults he filled with tribute.

- The Menagerie is the oldest deck and his first home in exile.
- The baths sit above cloning facilities; he calls the dead raw materials.
- The ship grows and changes with him. He treats that as renewal, not corruption.
- In Season of the Haunted it returns as the Derelict Leviathan, infected by
  Nightmares. Calus is absent, but his voice and architecture remain.
- The Leviathan is where he tests Guardians, collects species and stages his own
  idea of empire.
